## 📖 Texto Completo em Inglês
Every morning, I wake up early and head straight to the kitchen to brew fresh coffee. The rich aroma instantly helps me wake up and start my day with positive energy. I sit near the window, take slow sips, and plan my daily schedule before starting to work.
